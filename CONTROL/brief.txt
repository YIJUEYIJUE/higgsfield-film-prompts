In a near future, world population has reached ten billion — the maximum limit allowed. This is CONTROL. The story of a man who spends his life executing the law, and one second breaking it.

🎭 Narrative

CONTEXT

The project starts from a question that is no longer science fiction: what happens when the survival of the species is used to justify deciding who is allowed to be born. We are already building systems that register, score, and authorize human beings, and we are already comfortable letting them decide — as long as someone else carries out the sentence.  

AUDIENCE

Adults 18–45, core 25–40 — viewers of grounded, character-driven dystopian sci-fi in the vein of Children of Men and District 9.  It speaks to viewers interested in bioethics, state control, and reproductive rights told through image and restraint rather than exposition. Festival and streaming audiences receptive to short-form high-concept sci-fi with a strong visual identity.
