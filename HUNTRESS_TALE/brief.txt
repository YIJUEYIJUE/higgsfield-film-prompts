This is a project for animated short film Huntress Tale.

Logline: A lone huntress descends into a crumbling gothic ruin to hunt monsters only to be hunted herself by something far older, and betrayed by the one thing that tried to save her.
About the project:

Huntress's Tale is an animated short film in the action fantasy genre.Every kill makes you stronger. The huntress knows this law well, and she has been feeding on the temple's monsters without mercy.
Chasing a fleeing creature, the floor collapses and drops her into a flooded crypt — where something vast and eyeless waits, holding more power than she could ever take. For the first time, she runs.
A tiny pink angel appears and guides her to a hidden room. She shuts the door and breathes, safe at last. Behind her, the little angel begins to swell — and unfolds into the very monster she fled. She turns too late.
The hunter has become the harvest, and the hunt begins again.
