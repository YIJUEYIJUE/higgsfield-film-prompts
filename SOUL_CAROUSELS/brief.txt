An ongoing, curated collection of Soul 2.0 carousel posts, maintained as a living reference library.Unlike a fixed deliverable with a set end date, this collection grows continuously — every time the Soul Instagram account publishes a new carousel, it gets reviewed, curated, and added to the collection.


Every carousel is built around one idea or trend — that single concept is the spine the whole set hangs off. A carousel lives or dies on consistency across slides — same character/identity, same style, same lighting logic, same color world — while each slide still earns its own moment and advances the one idea being unpacked.
