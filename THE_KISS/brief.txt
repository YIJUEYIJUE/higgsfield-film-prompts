One brother got the kiss. The other got a phone ringing — and a nightmare built out of everything he was afraid it meant.
✨ Context
This project explores a feeling that all of us have experienced and understand: jealousy.

Sometimes, a person can become jealous of you even when you have done absolutely nothing to them. And when that jealousy grows, it can slowly turn into hatred — to the point where they may even wish you were gone.

The truth is, that person is not necessarily evil. They are simply being consumed by jealousy.

That is what this entire film is about.

It shows how something as small as an action from an outside person — in this case, the mother — can trigger jealousy between two brothers and completely change their relationship.

What begins as two brothers simply playing a game together and trying to win gradually turns into conflict, resentment, and eventually a situation where one of them wants to get rid of the other.

At its core, the film is a direct exploration of jealousy: how such a simple emotion can grow into something destructive, change the way we think, influence the choices we make, control the way we behave, and ultimately change the course of our lives.
